## 1. Overview & Core Value Proposition
**Parivahan Next** is a ground-up reimagining of India's transport infrastructure portals (previously fragmented across *Vahan*, *Sarathi*, *mParivahan*, and *eChallan*).

### What Has Changed?
- **Zero Portal Hopping**: Access all your registered cars, two-wheelers, driving licences, active permits, and pending challans from a single screen.
- **Plain-Language Interface**: Bureaucratic form numbers (Form 20, Form 29/30, Form 34, Form 35) are replaced with intuitive guided tasks ("Sell My Car", "Renew License", "Clear Loan NOC").
- **10 Indian Languages**: Native translations available in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, and English.
- **WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility**: High-contrast mode, dynamic font scaling, and voice screen reader for elderly or visually impaired citizens.
- **Fail-Safe Sessions**: Continuous auto-save saves form drafts locally so you never lose entered data upon connection drops.

## 2. Single-Sign-On & Unified Citizen Identity

Citizens can sign in using any of the following 3 verified authentication methods:
1. **Aadhaar-Linked OTP**: Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number to receive a secure 6-digit OTP. Your name, photo, address, and all registered transport assets are fetched automatically from UIDAI.
2. **Mobile OTP**: Enter your Aadhaar-registered mobile number to receive a one-touch SMS login code.
3. **DigiLocker Single-Sign-On**: Log in directly via your national DigiLocker credentials to sync verified digital credentials.

## 3. Citizen Dashboard ("Digital Garage") Guide

Upon logging in, citizens are greeted with their **Unified Digital Garage**:

### 3.1 Registered Vehicles Card Grid
- Displays authentic **High-Security Registration Plate (HSRP)** visuals.
- Displays 4 color-coded health badges:
  - **Insurance**: `VALID` (Green), `EXPIRING SOON` (Amber), `EXPIRED` (Red)
  - **PUC Pollution**: Real-time validity countdown and emission center code
  - **Fitness Certificate**: Statutory validity date
  - **Road Tax**: Lifetime or annual tax clearance receipt
- **Quick Action Menu**: 1-click access to *Transfer Ownership, Renew RC, Clear Loan NOC, Book Inspection, Download Virtual Smartcard*.

### 3.2 Driving Licence Smartcard Profile
- Interactive front/back smartcard preview with chip graphic, authorized vehicle classes (`MCWG`, `LMV`), blood group, and scannable QR verification code.

### 3.3 Live 5-Stage Application Tracker
Tracks every active request across 5 transparent milestones:
1. `Submitted` &rarr; Notice and form details recorded.
2. `Fee Paid` &rarr; BharatKosh statutory receipt generated.
3. `RTO Scrutiny` &rarr; Assigned officer name and live scrutiny remarks.
4. `Test / Inspection` &rarr; Scheduled appointment at automated sensor track.
5. `Approved & Issued` &rarr; Cryptographically signed digital certificate dispatched to your Document Vault.

## 4. Step-by-Step Vehicle Services

### 4.1 Transfer of Vehicle Ownership (Buyer-Seller Flow)
1. **Seller Initiation**: Choose the vehicle from your garage and enter the buyer's Name, Mobile, and Aadhaar number.
2. **Buyer Handshake**: The buyer receives an automated SMS consent notification and verifies the agreed sale price via OTP.
3. **Form 29 & 30 Digital Auto-Generation**: The system populates official Central Motor Vehicles Rules transfer notices with Aadhaar e-Signs.
4. **Fee Checkout**: Pay statutory transfer fee of ₹530 via UPI QR, NetBanking, or Card.
5. **RTO Scrutiny**: The application is routed to the RTO Officer for digital approval.

### 4.2 15-Year RC Renewal & Fitness Inspection
1. Select your 15+ year old vehicle.
2. Select an automated inspection track date and counter slot at your designated RTO.
3. Pay statutory renewal and inspection fee (₹1,250).
4. Download your QR Entry Pass and present your vehicle on the chosen date.

### 4.3 Hypothecation Addition & Loan NOC Termination
- **Loan Clearance (Form 35)**: Select vehicle, choose financing bank (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.), enter loan account number, and upload bank digital NOC. RTO fee: ₹350.
- **Loan Addition (Form 34)**: Endorse bank lien upon purchasing vehicle via auto financing. RTO fee: ₹550.

### 4.4 VIP Choice Number Plate Booking
- Search open registration digits (e.g. `0001`, `0007`, `9999`, `8055`).
- Reserve your number and pay booking fee to receive an instant 90-day allotment certificate for dealer HSRP embossing.

## 5. Step-by-Step Driving Licence Services

### 5.1 Applying for Learner’s Licence (e-KYC & Online Test)
1. **Paperless e-KYC**: Confirm pre-populated UIDAI details.
2. **Class Selection**: Choose `MCWG` (Motorcycles), `LMV` (Cars), or both.
3. **Interactive Mock Test**: Take a 15-question road safety, traffic sign, and regulatory test with live 10-minute timer.
4. **Instant Issuance**: Score 9/15 (60%) or higher, pay ₹250 fee, and instantly receive your provisional **Form 3 Learner’s Licence** valid for 6 months across India.

### 5.2 Converting to Permanent Driving Licence
1. Eligible 30 days after receiving your Learner's Licence.
2. Select an appointment date for the Automated Sensor Driving Track at your RTO.
3. Pay ₹650 statutory and smartcard postal fee.

### 5.3 International Driving Permit (IDP) Application
1. Enter destination country (USA, UK, UAE, Schengen, Australia, etc.), Passport Number, and Visa type.
2. Pay statutory fee of ₹1,050 to receive a 1-year valid 1949 Geneva Convention International Driving Permit.

## 6. Document Vault & DigiLocker Sync
- Stores cryptographically verified copies of:
  - Smartcard Registration Certificate (RC)
  - Permanent Driving Licence
  - Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate
  - Motor Vehicle Comprehensive Insurance
  - Road Tax Receipts
- **1-Click DigiLocker Sync**: Automatically updates credentials directly with the National Register.
- **Print / PDF Download**: Generate official printable slips compliant with Rule 139 CMVR.

## 7. BharatKosh Payment Gateway & Tax Invoices
- Supports:
  - **Dynamic UPI QR Code**: Scan and pay with any UPI App (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).
  - **UPI VPA / ID**: Send collect request directly to your mobile app.
  - **Internet Banking**: Over 50+ nationalized and private Indian banks.
  - **Debit / Credit Cards**: RuPay, Visa, and MasterCard.
- **Instant Tax Invoice**: Download signed BharatKosh government receipt with transaction hash and QR code.
- **Automatic Failure Recovery**: Built-in 1-click retry and transaction status lookup.

## 8. RTO Appointment Booking & QR Entry Pass
- Filter RTO offices by state and district.
- Pick real-time available morning or afternoon counter slots.
- Generate an express **QR Check-in Pass** for fast token issuance at the RTO reception desk.

## 9. Public Services Hub (No Login Required)
- **Know Your Vehicle**: Search any Indian registration number for masked owner details and roadworthiness status.
- **eChallan Search & Pay**: Look up pending traffic fines by Vehicle No or Challan No, view high-definition camera evidence snapshots, and clear fines online.
- **Verify Driving Licence**: Authenticate driver details and authorized vehicle classes.
- **RTO Locator & Map**: Addresses, working hours, and phone directory of all transport offices.
- **Service Fee Estimator**: Estimate statutory fees and road taxes.

## 10. RTO Officer Portal & Scrutiny Workflow
- **Application Queue**: Live workload tracker sorted by submission date and SLA deadline.
- **Split-Screen Scrutiny Desk**:
  - Left: Citizen credentials, vehicle specs, and BharatKosh tax invoice.
  - Right: High-resolution document viewer with verification badges.
  - Controls: *Approve & Sign Digitally*, *Reject Application*, or *Request Document Re-upload*.
- **Sensor Track Test Score Entry**: Record Pass/Fail decisions directly into the candidate's permanent DL file.
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Daily throughput, average disposal time, and revenue audits.

## 12. Grievance Redressal (CPGRAMS)
- Lodge citizen grievances regarding dispatch delays, test track queries, or officer conduct.
- Track resolution progress with a guaranteed 7-day Citizen Charter SLA deadline.
- Emergency Road Helplines: **1033** (National Highways) and **1073** (Transport Helpline).
